About Me

Born in Romania, I have spent the last 13 years abroad, and have called Czechia my home for the past 11.

What I learned as a long time expat and devourer of knowledge, is that civilization is the result of efffective human communication and cooperation.

Combining my practical knowledge as a scientist and Toastmaster with the wisdom of Eastern philosophies like Buddhism and Taoism, I came up with a practical philosophy of communication.

In the past 2 years, I applied and tested this philosophy over more than 20 discussion meetings as a host and over twice as many as an attendee.

I invite you to get to know more and join me in making the world a little bit nicer place to be.
